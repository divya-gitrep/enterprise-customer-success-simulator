# Enterprise Customer Success Simulator

## Overview

Enterprise Customer Success Simulator is an interactive web application that demonstrates how a Technical Success Manager (TSM) guides an enterprise customer from onboarding to renewal by leveraging observability, business value realization, adoption strategies, retention planning, and expansion opportunities.

The project simulates the lifecycle of a strategic enterprise customer and showcases how technical insights can be translated into measurable business outcomes.

---

## Problem Statement

Organizations invest heavily in observability platforms, but many struggle to:

* Achieve value quickly after onboarding
* Improve observability maturity
* Reduce incident resolution times
* Demonstrate business impact
* Maintain platform adoption
* Identify churn risks early
* Discover expansion opportunities

Technical Success Managers play a critical role in helping customers achieve these outcomes.

This project demonstrates how a TSM can proactively guide customers throughout their success journey.

## Features

### 1. Customer Discovery Workshop

Understand business goals, technical challenges, executive concerns, and success metrics.

### 2. Observability Maturity Assessment

Evaluate the customer's current observability capabilities across:

* Infrastructure Monitoring
* Application Performance Monitoring (APM)
* Distributed Tracing
* Alert Coverage
* Dashboard Adoption

### 3. First Value Plan

A 30-day onboarding roadmap designed to accelerate time-to-value through:

* Infrastructure onboarding
* Application instrumentation
* Dashboard creation
* Executive value reviews

### 4. Incident Command Center

Simulates a real-world production incident and demonstrates:

* Alert investigation
* Trace analysis
* Infrastructure analysis
* Root cause identification
* Resolution workflow

### 5. Business Value Dashboard

Shows how observability improvements translate into business outcomes.

Examples include:

* MTTR reduction
* SLA improvements
* Incident reduction
* Cost savings
* Revenue protection

### 6. Account Health Engine

Tracks:

* User engagement
* Dashboard adoption
* Instrumentation coverage
* Platform consumption
* Executive engagement

Generates an overall account health score.

### 7. Churn Risk Analysis

Identifies risk indicators and recommends proactive intervention strategies.

### 8. Expansion Opportunity Engine

Highlights opportunities to increase customer value through additional observability capabilities.

Examples:

* Browser Monitoring
* Mobile Monitoring
* Distributed Tracing

### 9. Executive Business Review (QBR)

Generates executive-ready summaries including:

* Achievements
* Risks
* ROI
* Strategic recommendations
* Next-quarter priorities

### 10. TSM Decision Simulator

Interactive scenarios that demonstrate how Technical Success Managers make strategic decisions to improve adoption, retention, and customer outcomes.
